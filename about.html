The Firm

About the firm

K.K. Muchangi & Co. Advocates is a law firm based in Ongata Rongai, Kenya, providing professional, responsive and practical legal services to businesses, institutions and private individuals.

The firm acts in both commercial and personal legal matters. It is committed to diligence in its work, to protecting the confidentiality of client information and to giving practical legal guidance that clients can understand and act on.

Our Approach

Guiding principles

Professionalism

Every matter is handled with care, diligence and respect for the profession.

Confidentiality

Information shared with the firm is treated with discretion.

Practicality

Advice is clear and aimed at workable outcomes.

Responsiveness

Integrity

The firm acts honestly and in line with its professional duties.

Why the Firm Exists

Clear guidance, responsibly given

Legal questions affect livelihoods, institutions and families. People and organisations need advice they can understand, from an advocate who is accountable for it.

The firm exists to provide that: clear explanations, honest assessment of the options and careful handling of every matter entrusted to it.
