Privacy Policy

How personal information received through this website is handled.

Who we are

This website is operated by K.K. Muchangi & Co. Advocates, Muungano Women's Building, 3rd Floor, Suite C7, Magadi Road, Ongata-Rongai. Contact: kkmuchangiadvocates24@gmail.com, 0758 522 687. This policy is based on the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya.

Information we collect

Through the enquiry form: your name, email address, telephone number, the general nature of your enquiry, preferred contact method and your message. If you contact us by telephone, WhatsApp or email, we receive the details you provide. The website itself does not require you to create an account.

Why we collect it

To respond to your enquiry, to carry out preliminary checks (such as conflict checks) before accepting instructions, and to meet our professional and legal obligations. We do not use enquiry information for unrelated marketing.

How enquiries are handled

Enquiries are received by the firm and read by authorised persons. Submitting an enquiry does not create an advocate-client relationship. Please do not send confidential documents, sensitive personal information or detailed allegations through the form or WhatsApp.

Retention

We keep enquiry information only for as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law or professional rules. Retention periods will be confirmed by the firm.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal data. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, which is why we ask you not to send confidential material through the website form or WhatsApp.

Third-party services

This website uses Google Fonts and an embedded Google Map, which may receive technical information such as your IP address when the page loads. If you use WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own terms and privacy notice apply. When the enquiry form is connected, an email or form-processing provider will handle submissions on our behalf.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask to be informed of the use of your personal data, to access it, to object to its processing, and to have inaccurate data corrected or deleted, subject to the Act. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Privacy enquiries

To exercise your rights or ask about this policy, email kkmuchangiadvocates24@gmail.com or telephone 0758 522 687.

The information on this website is general and is not legal advice. It is not a substitute for advice on your individual matter. Contacting the firm or submitting an enquiry does not create an advocate-client relationship.
