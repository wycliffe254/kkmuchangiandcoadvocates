The Firm

Your Legal Interests. Our Professional Responsibility.

K.K. Muchangi & Co. Advocates is a Kenyan law firm based in Ongata Rongai. The firm advises and represents businesses, institutions and individuals on both commercial and personal legal matters.

Each matter is approached with diligence and handled in confidence, with a focus on clear, practical guidance.

Our Approach

Five principles

Professionalism

Every matter is handled with care, diligence and respect for the profession.

Confidentiality

Information shared with the firm is treated with discretion.

Practicality

Advice is clear and aimed at workable outcomes.

Responsiveness

Enquiries are acknowledged and clients are kept informed.

Integrity

The firm acts honestly and in line with its professional duties.

Why Engage the Firm

Understand the issue. Weigh the options.

Good legal guidance begins with understanding your situation. The firm listens, explains the legal position in plain language, sets out the options available to you and advises on a sensible way forward.

The decision always remains yours, and the firm will tell you plainly what the law does and does not allow.
