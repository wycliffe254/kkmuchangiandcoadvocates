FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

General information about contacting and engaging the firm.

What legal services does K.K. Muchangi & Co. Advocates provide?

The firm acts in corporate and commercial, employment and labour, civil litigation and dispute resolution, property and real estate, criminal, and succession and private client matters. See the Practice Areas page for details.

How can I contact the firm?

By telephone on 0758 522 687, by WhatsApp on the same number, by email at kkmuchangiadvocates24@gmail.com, or through the enquiry form on the Contact Us page.

Where is the firm's office located?

Muungano Women's Building, 3rd Floor, Suite C7, Magadi Road, Ongata-Rongai, next to KCB Bank.

Do I need an appointment before visiting?

Please contact the office before visiting so that it can confirm whether someone is available to meet you.

How can I request a consultation?

Contact the office by telephone, WhatsApp or email, or use the enquiry form, and briefly state the general nature of your matter. The office will advise on the next step.

Does the firm handle corporate and institutional legal matters?

Yes. The firm is available to support businesses, schools, small and medium enterprises, property managers and other institutions. Terms are discussed with each organisation.

How are professional fees determined?

Fees depend on the nature and scope of the matter and are discussed with the client and agreed with the firm before the firm acts. Please ask the office when you make contact.

Can I request a review of an existing agreement?

You may ask the office whether it can review an agreement. Please do not send the document until the office has confirmed how it should be shared.

What information should I prepare before contacting the firm?

A short summary of what the matter is about, who is involved, any deadlines you are aware of, and the best way to reach you. Please do not send confidential documents until asked to.

How does the firm handle client confidentiality?

Confidentiality is one of the firm's guiding principles, and information given to the firm is treated with discretion in line with the firm's professional obligations. See the Privacy Policy for how personal data from this website is handled.

Does sending an enquiry automatically establish an advocate-client relationship?

No. An enquiry does not create an advocate-client relationship. Instructions are subject to preliminary checks and formal acceptance by the firm.

Can I contact the firm through WhatsApp?

Yes, for general enquiries and to arrange contact. Please do not send confidential documents, sensitive personal information or detailed allegations over WhatsApp.

The information on this website is general and is not legal advice. It is not a substitute for advice on your individual matter. Contacting the firm or submitting an enquiry does not create an advocate-client relationship.
